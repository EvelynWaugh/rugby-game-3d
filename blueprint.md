# This is Drone (FPV/Bomber) Game 3d in React Three Fiber.

## Main Concept
Look at rugby-game.html - that is canvas 2d game. We must made with same concept 3d game, where we can throw munitions for enemies, and it looks like rugby ball. But instead of flying across square, we fly along double sidepath, the landscape changes from level, it can be road, it can be trail or over field with yellow corn and blue sky.
We should see statistic for level during the game, how is battery charged, how many rugby balls (munitions) was used, because we have limits.
Drone can be used as kamikadze, once catch up enemies - fleeing away pigs - they smashed them and killed. But our drone keep flying.

## Drone
- Drone model provided (models/drone)
- It can throw rugby balls

## Enemies
- Pigs, jammer, drones with a net which tries to catch you. If you get caught you lose 30% or your max hp and taken down to land for 3 sec, after that net is dissapeared and you are good to go again.
- Pig's model provided (models/pig). YOu can smash them like FPV. In model they stay on there back legs and hit you with weapon Once you approach to them closer, they stay on their 4 lefs and with manuevering try to escape from you.
